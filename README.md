# 🥑 FoodVerse AI 

**A Multimodal AI-Powered Food Intelligence Platform**

Modern food ecosystems require intelligent systems that can seamlessly analyze food across multiple dimensions—visual appearance, preparation processes, hygiene practices, and individual dietary needs. **FoodVerse AI** is a modular, full-stack application that integrates computer vision, video analysis, and Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning to deliver real-time insights across food recognition, freshness detection, restaurant hygiene monitoring, smart cooking assistance, and personalized nutrition planning.

## ⚡ Core Modules & Features

* **🍽️ Food Recognition & Nutrition:** Automatically identifies dishes from uploaded images and generates estimated macros (calories, protein, carbs, fats) and a health score.
* **🍎 Freshness Detection:** Analyzes visual characteristics (color, texture, discoloration) to classify produce and food as Fresh, Moderately Fresh, or Spoiled.
* **🧼 Restaurant Hygiene Monitor:** Evaluates kitchen environments (glove usage, hair-cap compliance, surface cleanliness) to ensure sanitary compliance.
* **👨‍🍳 Smart AI Chef:** Generates custom recipes based on pantry images, voice inputs, and text prompts with step-by-step instructions.
* **📅 Personalized Meal Planner:** Adapts to individual user profiles, biometric parameters, dietary goals, and allergies to create customized multi-day meal plans.
